       1. Document  Control & Purpose                                             
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
       1.1 Purpose of this Document                                               
                                                                                  
       This Concept Note defines the product vision, functional scope, user model, workflow logic, AI capabilities and
       technical architecture for a proposed multi-tenant Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) and Third-Party Risk
       Management (TPRM) platform.                                                
       It is written as a pre-SRS engineering brief. It is intentionally detailed enough for the development team to derive
                                                                                  
       epics, user stories, data models and API contracts, but stops short of screen-level UI specification and final
       technology lock-in, both of which should follow a design sprint and an architecture review.
                                                                                  
       1.2 Intended Audience                                                      
                                                                                  
        • Product Owner and Business Analyst — for backlog creation and story writing
        • Solution / Application Architect — for architecture decision records and tech-stack finalisation
                                                                                  
        • Engineering Leads (Backend, Frontend, AI/ML, DevSecOps) — for module ownership and estimation
        • QA Lead — for test strategy and acceptance criteria derivation          
        • Business / Pre-Sales — for positioning, pricing model and go-to-market  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
       1.3 What is In Scope and Out of Scope for this Note                        
                                                                                  
        In Scope of this Concept Note  Deliberately Deferred                      
        Product vision, personas, tenancy and role model Pixel-level UI/UX design and design system
                                                                                  
        Functional modules and business rules Final infrastructure sizing and cost model
        Evidence cross-mapping and AI scoring logic Detailed API request/response schemas
                                                                                  
        Audit lifecycle and state machines Commercial pricing and licensing tiers 
                                                                                  
        Indicative technical architecture and data model Vendor/OEM selection for LLM and OCR services
        Phased delivery roadmap and MVP definition Formal SRS, test plan and traceability matrix
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
       1.4 Design Reference                                                       
                                                                                  
       The product is benchmarked against established compliance automation platforms in the market for user-
       experience patterns, module coverage and automation depth. The intent is functional parity on core GRC
       automation, with clear differentiation on (a) the audit-firm-led service delivery model, (b) evidence intelligence
       and cross-framework reuse, and (c) AI-generated maturity assessment. No third-party product name, branding,
       workflow copy or proprietary content is to be reproduced in the product, its documentation or its marketing
       collateral.

       2. Executive Summary                                                       
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
       Most organisations today pursue multiple compliance frameworks in parallel — ISO 27001, PCI DSS, SOC 2, DPDP
       Act, RBI / SEBI / IRDAI directives, CERT-In guidelines, HIPAA, GDPR, NIST CSF. The underlying controls overlap
                                                                                  
       heavily, often by 60-80%, yet the evidence is collected, uploaded, chased and reviewed separately for each
       framework. The result is duplicated effort, audit fatigue, inconsistent evidence quality and a compliance posture
       that is known only at a point in time.                                     
       This platform addresses that problem with three structural ideas:          
                                                                                  
         1. A Unified Control Framework (UCF). Every framework control is normalised to a common internal control
           objective. Evidence is attached to the control objective, not to the framework. Adding a second framework
           therefore inherits, rather than re-collects, evidence.                 
                                                                                  
         2. An AI Evidence Intelligence Engine. When evidence is uploaded, the system classifies it, extracts key
           attributes (dates, scope, approver, asset coverage, signatures), scores its quality, and automatically
           proposes it against every other control it can satisfy — accepting it, flagging a partial gap, or rejecting it with
           a specific reason and a re-upload request.                             
                                                                                  
         3. A three-sided tenancy model. The platform is operated as GRC-as-a-Service: the platform operator
           onboards audit firms, audit firms onboard and assess auditee organisations, and auditee organisations
           manage their own internal teams, vendors and CISO-level reporting — all with strict data isolation.
                                                                                  
       2.1 Core Capability Summary

       2.2 Primary Differentiators to Protect in Build                            
                                                                                  
        • Upload once, comply many — measurable reduction in duplicate evidence effort, reported as a hard metric
          on the dashboard                                                        
                                                                                  
        • Evidence is scored, not just stored — quality and maturity ratings, not a binary attached/not-attached flag
        • The auditor and the auditee work in the same system — no email-and-spreadsheet handoff, with a full
          immutable trail                                                         
                                                                                  
        • Control-level least privilege — a team member sees only the specific control points assigned to them, and
          nothing else                                                            
        • Gap-to-risk-to-task automation — a rejected or partial control automatically becomes a risk entry and an
          owned remediation task

       4.1 Tenant Types                                                           
                                                                                  
       The platform supports three tenant classes. A tenant is the hard boundary of data isolation.
        Tenant Type  Who It Is           What It Owns                             
                                                                                  
        Platform Tenant The platform operator / GRC Framework content packs, questionnaire library, global
                     service provider    configuration, all audit firm tenants, licensing, platform
                                         telemetry                                
                                                                                  
        Audit Firm Tenant A CERT-In empanelled or Its auditors, its managers, its engagement portfolio, its
                     independent audit / consulting report templates, its onboarded auditee organisations
                     firm                                                         
        Auditee Organisation The client organisation being Its own users, controls, evidence, policies, risks,
        Tenant       assessed            vendors, dashboards                      
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
       An Auditee Organisation Tenant may exist in one of two modes, and this must be a first-class concept in the data
       model:                                                                     
                                                                                  
        • Firm-managed — created and linked to an Audit Firm Tenant which holds an active engagement. The firm
          can see the linked scope.                                               
        • Self-service — the organisation subscribes directly with no audit firm attached, and may later invite an audit
          firm to an engagement.                                                  
                                                                                  
       Critical rule: an audit firm can see an auditee's data only through an active, scoped Engagement record.
       Revoking or closing the engagement immediately removes visibility, while retaining the historical audit record
       for defensibility.

       Operations team, that team member on login must see exactly that control point and nothing else — not the
       framework tree, not other domains, not other members' evidence, not the organisation score.
                                                                                  
       Implementation requirement:                                                
                                                                                  
        • A ControlAssignment record is the sole grant of access for the Control Owner role
        • Authorisation is evaluated server-side on every read and write against the assignment set
        • The assignment carries a scope: the control, the specific evidence requirements within it, a due date, and an
          optional expiry                                                         
                                                                                  
        • Removing an assignment immediately removes access, but retains the historical contribution in the audit
          trail                                                                   
        • A team member may be assigned control points across multiple frameworks; the system shows the

       9.4 Versioning, Supersession and Re-mapping                                
       This is the behaviour requested for the re-upload cycle and must be implemented precisely:
                                                                                  
        • Evidence is immutable. An update creates version N+1 and marks version N as SUPERSEDED; nothing is
          overwritten or hard-deleted                                             
                                                                                  
        • When version N+1 is accepted, the system automatically re-propagates it to every control link that version N
          held, across every framework                                            
        • Each inherited link is re-validated independently — the new version may satisfy some controls and fail others
          (for example, a refreshed policy that dropped a clause)                 
                                                                                  
        • Any link that now fails generates a new gap and a new task; any link that now passes closes its gap
          automatically                                                           
        • Controls already LOCKED by an auditor are excluded from automatic re-propagation and instead raise a
          change notification to the auditor                                      
                                                                                  
        • The version chain, the diff between versions, and the propagation result are all visible in the Evidence History
          timeline                                                                
                                                                                  
       9.5 Evidence History Timeline (UI requirement)                             
                                                                                  
       Every control point must show a chronological, filterable, exportable timeline containing: upload events with
       actor and timestamp, version changes with a visual diff, AI mapping decisions with reasons, auditor comments
       and queries, auditee responses, status transitions, lock and unlock events, and re-mapping propagation results.
       This timeline is the defensibility artefact for the audit and must be exportable as a signed PDF.

       10. M5 —  AI Evidence Intelligence & Cross-Framework Reuse Engine          
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
       This is the flagship differentiator. The scenario it solves: an organisation certified to ISO 27001 now decides to
       pursue PCI DSS. Instead of re-uploading the same 200 documents, the platform evaluates the existing evidence
                                                                                  
       pool against the new framework and tells the organisation exactly what it can reuse, what is stale, what is
       insufficient, and what is genuinely missing.                               
                                                                                  
       10.1 Engine Pipeline                                                       
                                                                                  
       New framework subscribed OR new evidence uploaded                          
                |                                                                 
         [1] CANDIDATE RETRIEVAL -> semantic + mapping-graph search over evidence pool
                |                                                                 
         [2] FRESHNESS VALIDATION -> dates vs. control validity window & audit period
                                                                                  
                |                                                                 
         [3] SCOPE VALIDATION -> entities/locations/systems vs. new framework scope
                |                                                                 
         [4] SUFFICIENCY VALIDATION -> required attributes & delta conditions of target control
                |                                                                 
         [5] QUALITY GATE    -> Evidence Quality Score vs. minimum threshold      
                                                                                  
                |                                                                 
         [6] VERDICT + EXPLANATION -> ACCEPT / PARTIAL / REJECT, with reason & required action
                |                                                                 
         [7] ACTION GENERATION -> gap record, task, owner, due date, re-upload request
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
       10.2 Stage Detail                                                          
                                                                                  
       Stage 1 — Candidate Retrieval                                              
                                                                                  
        • Primary path: the mapping graph. If evidence satisfies UCO-IAM-004 and the new framework's requirement
          maps to UCO-IAM-004, it is an immediate candidate — deterministic, fast, explainable
        • Secondary path: semantic search. Evidence chunk embeddings are matched against the target control
          objective embedding to catch evidence that was filed under a different control but is relevant
                                                                                  
        • Output: ranked candidate list with a mapping confidence score           
                                                                                  
       Stage 2 — Freshness Validation                                             
       Each EvidenceRequirement carries a validity_period_days. The engine compares the evidence's effective date
       against the target framework's audit period and today's date.

        period                                                                    
        Within window but expires before audit PASS WITH WARNING Flag for refresh with target date
        end date                                                                  
                                                                                  
        Expired or outside audit period FAIL — STALE Re-upload request with exact reason and

       11. M6 —  Compliance Score, Maturity Score & Evidence Quality Score        
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
       The product must produce three distinct and separately explainable scores. Conflating them is a common design
       error.                                                                     
                                                                                  
        Score            Question It Answers           Scale                      
                                                                                  
        Compliance Score Are the required controls in place and evidenced? 0-100% 
        Maturity Score   How well established, repeatable and measured is the 0-5 (CMMI-style)
                         practice?

       11.1 Compliance Score                                                      
       Computed bottom-up from control status, with weights for criticality.      
                                                                                  
       Control weight w(c) = criticality_factor (Critical 3, High 2, Medium 1.5, Low 1)
       Status value v(c) = Compliant 1.0 | Partially Compliant 0.5 | Non-Compliant 0.0
                       Not Applicable -> excluded from both numerator and denominator
                       Not Assessed -> counted as 0 in denominator (visible as 'unassessed
       drag')                                                                     
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
       Domain Compliance % = 100 x SUM( w(c) x v(c) ) / SUM( w(c) ) for controls in domain
       Framework Compliance % = 100 x SUM( w(c) x v(c) ) / SUM( w(c) ) for controls in framework
       Org Compliance % = weighted mean of framework scores, framework weights configurable
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
       Rule: any Critical control in Non-Compliant status caps the framework score at a configurable ceiling (default
       70%) and is flagged as a blocking gap, so a single fatal failure cannot be averaged away.

       24. Delivery Roadmap

        • Identity, RBAC, MFA and audit logging framework                         
                                                                                  
        • UCF data model plus Content Studio for framework pack authoring         
        • Ingestion of the first two framework packs (ISO 27001:2022 and PCI DSS v4.0) with expert-authored
          mappings                                                                
                                                                                  
       24.2 Phase 1 — MVP (Weeks 7-22)                                            
                                                                                  
       Goal: a complete, demonstrable single-framework audit cycle plus the reuse engine on a second framework.
                                                                                  
        • Tenant, user and organisation onboarding; applicability wizard; scope definition
        • Control workspace; control assignment to team members with strict scoped visibility
                                                                                  
        • Evidence upload pipeline with classification, OCR, attribute extraction and versioning
        • AI cross-mapping engine — candidate retrieval, freshness, scope, sufficiency, verdict and gap generation
        • Compliance score, maturity score and evidence quality score             
                                                                                  
        • Auditor workspace — engagement, allocation, AI audit brief, control review, verdicts, findings, locking
        • Basic CISO dashboard; task engine; notifications; core reports (gap assessment, audit report, SoA)
                                                                                  
        • Third and fourth framework packs (SOC 2, DPDP) to prove the reuse claim end to end
                                                                                  
       24.3 Phase 2 — Expand (Weeks 23-38)                                        
                                                                                  
        • Third-Party Risk Management module in full — vendor register, tiering, questionnaire builder, vendor portal,
          AI response analysis, scoring, scorecards                               
        • Risk register with AI-generated risks, treatment workflow and heat maps 
                                                                                  
        • Policy management with AI review, drafting, approval workflow and attestation campaigns
        • Full CISO Portal with G/R/C pillars, trends, alerting and board pack generation
        • First integration wave: Entra ID/Okta, AWS, Azure, Jira, GitHub         
                                                                                  
        • Custom report builder; scheduled reports; white-labelling for audit firms
                                                                                  
       24.4 Phase 3 — Differentiate (Weeks 39-56)                                 
                                                                                  
        • Continuous vendor monitoring with external intelligence signals         
        • Conversational compliance assistant across controls, evidence and policies
                                                                                  
        • Additional framework packs and a custom framework builder for clients   
        • Second integration wave: ITSM, HRMS, endpoint, vulnerability, SIEM, backup
        • Peer benchmarking, predictive readiness forecasting, anomaly detection  
                                                                                  
        • Native mobile apps; multi-entity group consolidation; marketplace and public API programme

       Appendix  A — Glossary                                                     
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
        Term         Definition                                                   
                                                                                  
        UCO          Unified Control Objective — the normalised internal control statement to which all
                     framework requirements map                                   
                                                                                  
        UCF          Unified Control Framework — the complete library of UCOs and their cross-framework
                     mappings                                                     
        Content Pack A versioned, machine-readable bundle of a framework's requirements, mappings and
                     evidence requirements                                        
                                                                                  
        Engagement   A scoped, time-bound audit relationship between an audit firm tenant and an auditee tenant
        Evidence Link The association between an evidence artefact and a control, carrying the verdict and its
                     reason                                                       
                                                                                  
        Delta Condition A machine-evaluable parameter a stricter framework adds over the base control objective
        Gap          A specific, actionable shortfall identified by the engine or by an auditor
                                                                                  
        Finding      A formally raised audit non-conformity or observation, classified and tracked to closure
        Lock         The state in which an auditee can no longer modify a control because the auditor has closed it
                                                                                  
        Reuse Rate   The proportion of evidence links created by inheritance rather than by fresh upload
                                                                                  
        Tier (vendor) The criticality classification that determines assessment depth and cadence
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
       Appendix  B — Consolidated Business Rules Checklist                        
                                                                                  
       A quick reference for developers and QA. Every item here must have a corresponding test case.
                                                                                  
         53. A Control Owner can read and write only controls explicitly assigned to them; every other control returns
           403 at the API                                                         
                                                                                  
         54. An audit firm can access auditee data only through an active engagement; closing the engagement revokes
           access immediately                                                     
         55. Evidence is never overwritten; updates create a new version and supersede the previous one
         56. An accepted new evidence version re-propagates to every control the previous version was linked to, and
                                                                                  
           each link is re-validated independently                                
         57. Controls locked by an auditor are excluded from automatic re-propagation and instead notify the auditor of
           the change                                                             
         58. A control marked Compliant or closed by an auditor becomes read-only to the auditee at the API layer
                                                                                  
         59. Unlocking requires an authorised role, a mandatory reason, and is recorded in the audit log and the report
           exceptions                                                             
         60. Evidence that fails freshness, scope or sufficiency validation is never silently linked; it produces a specific,
                                                                                  
           actionable re-upload request                                           
         61. AI never records a final audit verdict; it proposes and explains, and a human decides
         62. Every AI decision stores its model, version, prompt version, retrieved sources and confidence
                                                                                  
         63. A Critical control in Non-Compliant status caps the framework compliance score at the configured ceiling
         64. Not Applicable status requires a mandatory documented justification and is excluded from scoring
